PANCAKES!

Description:

Pancakes, need we say more?

Enjoy steaming with butter and syrup.

Ingredients:

  • Milk
  • Eggs
  • Sugar
  • Flour
  • Baking Powder

Process:

  1. Sift dries together and combine with wets.
  2. Mix to smooth batter; do not overmix.
  3. Heat pan to medium heat.
  4. Add butter or oil of choice, cook pancakes by adding 1/3 cup batter per cake.
  5. Flip as edges brown, cook 1-2 more min, remove to plate to serve.
