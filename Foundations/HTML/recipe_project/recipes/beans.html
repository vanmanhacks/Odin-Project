BEANS!

Description:

Slow-cooked Belizean beans flavored with garlic, fresh pepper, and local onion. Slow cooking releases flavor, thickening starch-like substances, and proteinous fiber from the beans.

Ingredients:

  • Beans
  • Water
  • Soy Sauce
  • Rice Wine Vinegar
  • Chipotle Tabasco Sauce

Process:

  1. Pour canned beans into pot, do not rinse goop from beans.
  2. Add water, soy sauce, vinegar, tabasco sauce.
  3. Cook med-high heat until boil is achieved.
  4. After boil, adjust heat to med-low.
  5. Stir to thicken.
